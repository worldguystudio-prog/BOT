===============================================================================
                  ORGVNUM — HOW EACH TEAM WORKS
            Plain-English guides for every role in the server
===============================================================================

Hey! This folder has a separate guide for each role/department in ORGVNUM.
Pick the one that matches your role and read it — it'll tell you exactly what
you can do, what you can't do, and how to use the bot to do your job.

If you're not sure where you fit, read them all — they're short.


GUIDES IN THIS FOLDER:
─────────────────────

  01_Owner_and_Administrators.txt
      For the server owner and full admins. Covers /dashboard, /config,
      /sync, /database, /debug, and overall server management.

  02_Directorate_Command.txt
      For senior command staff. Covers announcements, oversight, and
      everything moderators and recruiters can do.

  03_Moderators.txt
      For the mod team. Covers warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, timeouts,
      purges, locks, case lookups, and automod.

  04_Recruiters.txt
      For the recruitment team. Covers applications, the waitlist,
      interviews, and applicant decisions.

  05_Trainers.txt
      For training staff. Covers creating events, tracking attendance,
      and awarding points.

  06_Staff_General.txt
      For general staff. Covers tickets, shifts, roleplay basics,
      and the everyday tools every staff member uses.

  07_Tickets_and_Support.txt
      For anyone working tickets. Covers the ticket panel, claiming,
      transcripts, adding/removing users, and closing tickets properly.

  08_Roleplay_Personnel.txt
      For roleplay staff. Covers personnel files, callsigns, dispatch,
      scenes, alerts, and department assignments.

  09_Shifts_and_Activity.txt
      For everyone on the staff team. Covers starting/ending shifts, the
      shift leaderboard, and how activity is tracked.

  10_Economy_and_Points.txt
      For staff who award points. Covers /points, /balance, and the
      leaderboard. (Optional system — only if enabled.)


HOW TO USE THESE GUIDES:
──────────────────────

1. Find your role's guide (or read the general Staff one if you're not sure).
2. Skim it once — it'll take 5 minutes.
3. Keep it open in a tab while you work until the commands are muscle memory.

Every guide lists the exact commands you can use, what each one does, and
the gotchas to watch out for. If you get stuck, ask in your staff channel
or check the main HOW_TO_USE.txt in the root of the repo.

Welcome to the team. Run a tight ship. 🛡️
