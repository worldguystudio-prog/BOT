===============================================================================
              ORGVNUM — ECONOMY & POINTS GUIDE
            (For staff who award points)
===============================================================================

The points system is OPTIONAL — an admin has to enable it via
/dashboard → Economy → Economy Enabled → Enable.

If it's disabled, the /points command still works (it stores points
internally), but they don't do anything visible to members until the
economy is turned on.

This guide covers how to award points, check balances, and run the
leaderboard.


WHAT POINTS ARE FOR
──────────────────

Points reward members for contributing to ORGVNUM. Members earn points by:
  • Attending training sessions
  • Participating in events / operations / patrols
  • Approved roleplay activities
  • Staff contributions (handled by trainers/admins)
  • Going above and beyond

Points are NOT currency. You can't buy anything with them. There's no
gambling, no shop, no transfer between members. They're a recognition
system — a way to say "you showed up and did the work."

The leaderboard is the payoff: members see their name climb as they
contribute. That's it. Simple and clean.


AWARDING POINTS (staff only)
────────────────────────────

  /points user:@someone amount:10 reason:Attended Basic Training #1
  /points user:@someone amount:25 reason:Ran a great RP scene
  /points user:@someone amount:50 reason:Helped onboard 3 new members
  /points user:@someone amount:-5 reason:Warning: missed scheduled event

Rules:
  • Positive amounts add points. Negative amounts remove them.
  • Always include a clear reason. It's saved to the transaction log.
  • Be consistent — if you always give 10 for attending training, stick
    with that. Don't play favorites.
  • Use negative amounts sparingly and only for legitimate reasons
    (e.g. points were awarded by mistake, or a member was caught
    gaming the system).


CHECKING BALANCES
────────────────

  /balance                       (your own balance — any member can use this)
  /balance user:@someone         (someone else's balance — staff only)


THE LEADERBOARD
───────────────

  /leaderboard                   (points leaderboard — top 10)
  /leaderboard type:points       (same thing, explicit)
  /leaderboard type:shifts       (shift leaderboard instead)

Shows the top 10 members by points:
  1. @user1 — 250 pts
  2. @user2 — 180 pts
  3. @user3 — 145 pts
  ...


HOW TO USE POINTS WELL
──────────────────────

  DO:
  • Award points immediately after the activity, while it's fresh.
    "Attended Basic Training #1" beats a generic reason days later.
  • Use round numbers (5, 10, 25, 50) for consistency.
  • Be transparent — tell members why they're getting points.
  • Recognize effort, not just outcomes. Someone who tried hard and
    learned something deserves points even if they struggled.

  DON'T:
  • Award points for showing up once. Reward contribution, not presence.
  • Use points as a punishment tool routinely. They're for recognition.
  • Give huge amounts casually. Inflation makes them meaningless.
  • Award points to yourself. That's an admin-only thing if it happens
    at all, and it should be transparent.


SUGGESTED POINT VALUES (calibrate as a team)
───────────────────────────────────────────

These are starting points — agree on values with your team and stick
to them:

  Attended a training session          10 pts
  Ran a training session (trainer)     25 pts
  Participated in an operation         15 pts
  Led an operation                     30 pts
  Helped onboard a new member           20 pts
  Submitted a quality RP scene         15 pts
  Caught a rule violation (verified)   10 pts
  Monthly activity bonus (top 3)       50 pts

Adjust to taste. The key is consistency.


WHAT YOU CAN'T DO:
─────────────────

  ❌ Transfer points between members (no transfer command by design)
  ❌ Gamble points (no gambling, period)
  ❌ Buy roles or perks with points (no shop, period)
  ❌ See the full transaction history (admin-only via /database)

  You CAN: award/remove points, check balances, see the leaderboard.


IF THE ECONOMY IS DISABLED
──────────────────────────

An admin can turn it off (or it was never turned on). In that case:
  • /points still works (saves to the database)
  • /balance still works (shows the saved total)
  • /leaderboard still works (shows the ranking)

But members won't see the economy as "active" — there's no in-server
indication it's on. The data is still being collected for when an admin
does enable it.

To enable: /dashboard → Economy → Economy Enabled → Enable.


TIPS:
────

  • Don't make it rain. Awarding 100 points for everything makes the
    system meaningless. Small, consistent awards build up over time.

  • Praise publicly. When you award points, mention it in the relevant
    channel: "Nice work on that scene, +15 pts." The recognition
    matters more than the number.

  • Review the leaderboard occasionally. If someone's grinding points
    without real contribution, talk to them. The leaderboard should
    reflect actual value to the community.

  • If you awarded points by mistake (wrong user, wrong amount), award
    a negative amount to correct it: /points user:@someone amount:-10
    reason:Correcting previous over-award


THE COMMANDS:
────────────

  /points user:@someone amount:10 reason:...
  /balance
  /balance user:@someone
  /leaderboard

Four commands. Award consistently, recognize effort, keep the
leaderboard honest.
