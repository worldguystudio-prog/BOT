===============================================================================
              ORGVNUM — RECRUITER GUIDE
                       (Permission Level 50)
===============================================================================

You're on the recruitment team. Your job is handling applications, running
the placement waitlist, and bringing new members into ORGVNUM. This guide
covers everything you need.


THE APPLICATION FLOW (how it works end-to-end)
──────────────────────────────────────────────

1. An admin posts the application panel in a channel:
     /application-panel channel:#applications

2. Members see buttons: [Recruitment] [Placement] [Staff] [Leadership]

3. They click a button → a form pops up (Discord username, age, timezone,
   experience, why ORGVNUM).

4. When they submit:
   • Their application is saved to the database with a unique ID (#0001, #0002…)
   • A review panel is posted to your configured application review channel
   • The applicant gets a hidden confirmation with their application ID
   • You (the recruiter) see the review panel in your review channel

5. You review it and click a button or use a command to decide.


VIEWING APPLICATIONS
────────────────────

  /applications                    — list recent applications
  /applications status:PENDING     — filter by status
  /application id:0042             — view one + see the review buttons

Statuses:
  PENDING → UNDER REVIEW → INTERVIEW → ACCEPTED / DENIED / WAITLISTED / CLOSED


MAKING DECISIONS
────────────────

You can use either the buttons on the review panel or the commands:

  /accept id:0042 notes:Great fit, welcome to the team
  /deny id:0042 notes:Not enough experience, reapply in 30 days
  /interview id:0042                  (moves to INTERVIEW status)
  /waitlist ...                       (moves to WAITLISTED — see below)

When you decide, the applicant gets a DM automatically with the new status
and your name. You don't need to message them separately.

The review panel buttons do the same thing as the commands:
  [ Accept ] [ Deny ] [ Waitlist ] [ Interview ] [ Request Info ]


THE PLACEMENT WAITLIST
──────────────────────

A separate queue for placement applications. Members submit:

  Roblox username, Discord username, age, timezone, activity level,
  previous experience, why they want to join, desired role, skills,
  availability.

TO JOIN (members do this themselves):
  /waitlist add
  (Opens a form — fills it out, gets an entry ID like #0005)

STAFF MANAGEMENT:
  /waitlist view                        — list all entries
  /waitlist view status:PENDING REVIEW  — filter by status
  /waitlist status user:@someone        — view one person's entry
  /waitlist promote id:5                — promote an entry to PROMOTED
  /waitlist remove id:5                 — remove an entry

Statuses: PENDING REVIEW → PROMOTED / REJECTED / CLOSED.

Important: We NEVER auto-accept anyone. You review every submission and
decide manually. Promoting doesn't accept them — it just flags them as
ready for the next step.


INTERVIEW SCHEDULING
───────────────────

There's no built-in calendar — when you move someone to INTERVIEW status,
DM them to schedule a time. Use your best judgment.

A typical interview flow:
  1. /interview id:0042 (moves them to INTERVIEW status)
  2. DM the applicant to schedule
  3. Conduct the interview in a voice channel or DM
  4. /accept id:0042 or /deny id:0042 based on the interview


REVIEWER NOTES
─────────────

The /accept and /deny commands take an optional notes parameter. These
notes are saved to the applicant's record. Use them to document:
  • Why you accepted/denied
  • What role they were accepted for
  • Conditions (e.g. "reapply in 30 days")
  • Interview notes

Other recruiters can see these notes via /application id:0042.


WHAT YOU CAN'T DO:
─────────────────

  ❌ /application-panel — admin only (posting the panel)
  ❌ /dashboard, /config — admin only
  ❌ Moderation commands (warn/mute/kick/ban) — that's moderators

  You CAN see applications and waitlist entries, but you can't moderate
  the server. If an applicant is being disruptive, ask a moderator.


TIPS:
────

  • Be responsive. Applicants are waiting on you. Try to review new
    submissions within 24-48 hours.

  • Be clear in your denial notes. "Reapply in 30 days with more RP
    experience" is more helpful than "denied."

  • Don't accept everyone to boost numbers. A small, active, quality
    membership beats a huge inactive one every time.

  • Watch for applicants who submit multiple times after being denied.
    Use /applications to check their history before deciding.


THE COMMANDS YOU'LL USE MOST:
─────────────────────────────

  /applications status:PENDING     (your daily to-do list)
  /application id:0042             (review one)
  /accept id:0042 notes:...        (decide)
  /deny id:0042 notes:...           (decide)
  /waitlist view                   (check the queue)

That's your workflow. Open /applications status:PENDING in the morning,
work through them, done.
