              ORGVNUM — STAFF (GENERAL) GUIDE
                       (Permission Level 30)
===============================================================================

You're on the staff team. This guide covers the everyday tools every
staff member uses — tickets, shifts, roleplay basics, and the utility
commands you'll reach for constantly.

If you have a specialized role (moderator, recruiter, trainer), read that
guide too. This one's the foundation.


TICKETS (your main job)
───────────────────────

Most staff work happens in tickets. Members open tickets to get help,
appeal warnings, report users, ask questions, etc.

POSTING THE PANEL (admin does this once):
  /ticket-panel channel:#support

When a member opens a ticket:
  • A private channel is created under the ticket category
  • You (staff) get access automatically
  • The member gets a welcome embed with Claim / Close / Lock / Rename buttons

MANAGING TICKETS:
  /claim                       (you take ownership of the ticket)
  /unclaim                     (release your claim)
  /adduser user:@someone       (add someone to this ticket)
  /removeuser user:@someone    (remove someone from this ticket)
  /rename name:new-name        (rename the ticket channel)
  /lockticket                  (stop the requester from sending messages)
  /unlockticket                (let them send messages again)
  /closeticket                 (generate transcript, post to transcript
                                channel, delete the ticket channel)

The buttons on the ticket message do the same things as the commands.

Read 07_Tickets_and_Support.txt for the full ticket guide.


SHIFTS (track your activity)
────────────────────────────

When you're working — handling tickets, running RP, helping members —
you should be on the clock. It feeds into the activity leaderboard.

  /shift start department:Support
  /shift end
  /shift status                  (see how long you've been on shift)
  /shift leaderboard             (see who's been putting in the most time)

Read 09_Shifts_and_Activity.txt for details.


ROLEPLAY BASICS
───────────────

Even if you're not in the roleplay team, you'll use some RP commands:

  /profile                       (view your personnel file)
  /profile user:@someone         (view someone else's)
  /status set:Active             (set your status: Active/On Leave/LOA/Inactive)

If you're assigned to a department, your callsign and department show on
your profile. Read 08_Roleplay_Personnel.txt if you handle roleplay stuff.


UTILITIES (the everyday tools)
──────────────────────────────

  /ping                          (is the bot alive? shows latency)
  /userinfo user:@someone        (member info — join date, roles, etc.)
  /serverinfo                    (server stats)
  /roleinfo role:@Staff          (role details)
  /avatar user:@someone          (view their avatar)
  /banner user:@someone          (view their banner)
  /botinfo                       (bot version, uptime, server count)
  /help                          (interactive command browser)


WHAT YOU CAN'T DO:
─────────────────

  ❌ Moderation commands (warn/mute/kick/ban) — that's moderators
  ❌ Application decisions (accept/deny) — that's recruiters
  ❌ Creating training events — that's trainers
  ❌ /dashboard, /config — admin only
  ❌ /announcement, /dispatch, /alert — Directorate and above

  You CAN: work tickets, run shifts, view profiles, use utilities.
  That's the bulk of day-to-day staff work.


TIPS:
────

  • Claim tickets before working them. It tells other staff "I've got
    this one" so you don't trip over each other.

  • Use /closeticket (or the Close button) when you're done. Don't
    leave tickets open — they clutter the category and confuse members.

  • Start a shift when you come online, end it when you leave. It's
    how we track who's active.

  • If a ticket is beyond your scope, ask in your staff channel. Don't
    guess — escalate.


THE COMMANDS YOU'LL USE MOST:
─────────────────────────────

  /claim /closeticket            (ticket workflow)
  /shift start /shift end       (clocking in and out)
  /profile                       (who am I looking at?)
  /ping                          (sanity check)
  /userinfo user:@someone        (member lookup)

Memorize those. They cover most of your day.


YOUR DAILY ROUTINE:
──────────────────

  1. Come online.
  2. /shift start department:Support
  3. Check the ticket category for open tickets.
  4. Claim one, handle it, close it.
  5. Repeat.
  6. /shift end before you leave.

Simple. Effective. Keep the queue clear.
